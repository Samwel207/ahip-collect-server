How to design a form

  1. + New form starts a blank form or a copy of a tool. Each section is one page on the phone.
  2. ＋ (toolbar on the right) adds a question after the open one: text, email, phone, multiple choice, checkboxes, dropdown, linear scale, Likert scale, grids (tables), number, slider, date, time, photo, audio, GPS or instructions.
  3. Click a question to edit it. More options holds Show only if (any answer: choice, number, text, date, “is answered”) and answer checks. Drag ⠿ to reorder.
  4. ⑂ Logic on a section, or Go to section based on answer on a multiple-choice question, skips whole pages.
  5. Preview fills the form as the data collector will, with all conditions working.
  6. Quiz (form settings): give each question its correct answer and points, and a time limit for the whole quiz. When time runs out the phone submits the answers given; the phone shows the score at the end and the dashboard shows scores per person.
  7. Or Upload Excel: download the template (it has a short guide sheet), fill it, upload, check the preview.
  8. Publish. Phones receive the new version at their next Sync. Earlier versions can be restored from History.

Name of CPP (Tools 1, 3, 5) is a dropdown of every CPP already submitted, plus “New CPP (not in the list)”. It updates itself every 30 minutes.

Items with a 🔒 are used by the app (site, region, GPS, recording, photos) and cannot be removed. Changing an option's wording is safe; deleting options or questions affects how old answers are read.
